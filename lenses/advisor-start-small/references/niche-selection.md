# Niche Selection & Market Evaluation — Start Small, Stay Small Reference

## Source
Rob Walling, "Start Small, Stay Small" (2010), Chapters 1-2.

## Core Principle: Market First Approach [SSSS:MF]

"Market comes first, marketing second, aesthetic third, and functionality a distant fourth." [SSSS:MF.1]

Продукт с большим рынком и низкой конкуренцией побеждает даже с плохим маркетингом, плохой эстетикой и слабым функционалом. Пример: QuickBooks в ранние годы. Любой нишевый продукт с ужасным сайтом, но тысячами продаж.

**Антитезис — Product First Approach (ОШИБКА):** Придумал приложение → 9 месяцев разработки → никто не покупает. "Building something no one wants is the most common source of failure for entrepreneurs." [SSSS:MF.2]

## Decision Algorithm: Выбор Ниши [SSSS:NS]

### APPLY WHEN:
- Нет идеи продукта (ищешь с нуля)
- Есть идея, но не проверен рынок
- Хочешь минимизировать risk

### AVOID WHEN:
- Уже есть PMF и платящие клиенты
- Ниша уже выбрана и валидирована данными

### COMBINES WITH:
- `[SSSS:DM]` Demand Measurement — количественная проверка ниши
- `[SSSS:PD]` Product — выбор типа продукта для ниши
- `[SSSS:MK]` Marketing — проверка наличия дешёвых каналов

### Step 1: Warm Niches [SSSS:NS.1]

"Warm niche" = ниша, где у тебя есть связь. Жена-юрист, брат-сантехник, хобби — коллекционирование вин.

**Упражнение:**
1. Две колонки: "Person" | "Hobby or Work Experience"
2. Заполнить для себя, друзей, родственников, коллег
3. Это твои warm niches — входная точка в рынки

**Почему важно:** "Introducing a product into this niche will be much easier than choosing a completely unknown market. You stand a better chance when you know who you're selling to."

### Step 2: Brainstorming [SSSS:NS.2]

**Approach #1 — Areas of Life:** Хобби, интересы, рабочий опыт. Спорт? Здоровье? Искусство?

**Approach #2 — Occupations:** Скан списков профессий (Bureau of Labor Statistics). Есть ли знакомый в этой профессии?

**Approach #3 — Cheat:** Пропустить brainstorming, сразу смотреть готовые идеи (entrepreneur.com/businessideas, YCombinator ideas). Адаптировать под свою warm niche.

**Ключевой инсайт:** "The best niches are under the radar, and you have to get out and do something before you will find them."

### Step 3: Нишевая Оценка [SSSS:NS.3]

**Два вопроса:**
1. Рынок достаточно большой?
2. Есть дешёвый способ до них достучаться?

## 6 Причин Выбирать Ниши [SSSS:NS.4]

### Reason #1: Узкий Фокус Продукта
"Would you rather sell your software to 100% of the people in a 5,000 person niche, or 10% of the people in a 50,000 person niche? Correct answer: 100% of 5,000." Дешевле рекламировать, выше концентрация, ниже конкуренция.

### Reason #2: Реклама Дешевле
Полная страница в Travel + Leisure = $106,000. В Northwest Magazine = $2,897 (36x дешевле). PPC: "travel" = $146-239K/мес. "Oregon travel" = $110-180/мес.

### Reason #3: Меньше Конкуренции
Nota Bene: word processor для академиков, $399. Microsoft не трогает рынки <$100M. "Owning the majority of a $500,000/year market is enough to support a very profitable startup."

### Reason #4: Выше Маржа
Nota Bene $399 vs Microsoft Word $79. Меньше конкурентов = можно заряжать больше. Нишевая утилита = willing to pay premium.

### Reason #5: Слабый Маркетинг Конкурентов
Нишевые рынки преследуются мелкими игроками без marketing expertise. Легче конкурировать чем с Super Bowl рекламой Monster.com.

### Reason #6: Легче Заслужить Доверие
В маленьком рынке тебя заметят 2-3 раза быстрее. "These days, hearing about anything more than once has become remarkable."

## Vertical vs Horizontal Markets [SSSS:MF.3]

**Vertical market** = одна отрасль/хобби (pool cleaners, web designers, wine collectors).
**Horizontal market** = пересекает отрасли (general invoicing software).

### Почему Vertical лучше:

1. **Схожее поведение** — один marketing message работает
2. **Общаются друг с другом** — word of mouth, thought leaders
3. **"Тусуются" вместе** — conferences, forums, online communities → easy to find
4. **Схожие потребности** — feasible для 1-2 person team удовлетворить ALL needs

**Horizontal markets**: "As a general rule, horizontal markets are too large and expensive for self-funded startups to navigate."

## Demand Measurement — Micropreneur Methodology [SSSS:DM]

### Conversion Rate Benchmarks [SSSS:DM.1]

| Price Range | Conversion Rate |
|-------------|----------------|
| $1-50 (consumer) | 1-4% |
| $50-1000 (recurring) | 0.5-2% |

### Traffic Formula [SSSS:DM.2]

```
Revenue = Visitors × Conversion Rate × Price

Пример: $200 product, 0.5% conversion
1,000 visitors × 0.005 × $200 = $1,000/мес
```

### Traffic Breakdown (rule of thumb) [SSSS:DM.3]

| Source | % |
|--------|---|
| Search Engines | ~33% |
| Incoming Links | ~33% |
| Direct Traffic | ~33% |

Если нужно 1,000 visitors total → нужно ~500 из organic search (with safety margin).

### Free Keyword Research Steps [SSSS:DM.4]

1. **Google AdWords Keyword Tool** — exact match, monthly searches
2. **Choose Keywords** — anchor + related terms
3. **SEO Logs Keyword Difficulty** — score <39 = good, 40-59 = so-so, >60 = bad
4. **Sanity Check** — manually check top Google results: PageRank, meta tags, domain age, backlinks

**Rule of thumb для AdWords data:** Показатели overestimate в ~2x. Берём половину.

### Micropreneur Methodology Formula [SSSS:DM.5]

```
Estimated Traffic = (AdWords Monthly Searches × 0.5) × 2 (long-tail) × 2 (other sources) = AdWords × 2

Пример: "attorney billing software" = 1,900 searches
Estimated traffic: 1,900 × 0.5 × 2 × 2 = 3,800 visitors/мес
At $500 price, 0.2% conversion: $3,800/мес revenue
```

## Mini Sales Site — Testing for Under $100 [SSSS:DM.6]

### APPLY WHEN:
- Прошёл keyword research, 1-4 product ideas осталось
- Хочешь проверить demand перед building

### Algorithm:

1. Выбрать самую интересную идею
2. Построить Mini Sales Site (2-3 страницы): Home, Tour (optional), Pricing & Sign-up
3. Home + Tour: screenshots (mockups от дизайнера, ~$300), feature list
4. Pricing page: "Buy Now" или "Download Free Trial" кнопка
5. Создать AdWords campaign → drive traffic
6. При клике на "Buy/Try" → track conversion + show "Coming Soon" + capture email
7. После 100-200 visitors → analyse numbers

### Conversion Expectations:
- "Download free trial" clicks → assume 2-5% would actually trial
- "Buy now" clicks → assume 40% would complete purchase

### Стоимость: $50-$500 (AdWords) + ~$300 (mockup design).

### НЕ работает для: Social networks, marketplace products (network effect required).

## Developer-to-Entrepreneur Transition [SSSS:DT]

### 9 Realizations [SSSS:DT.1-9]:

1. **Good technician is not enough** — E-Myth: entrepreneur + manager + technician. 95% of us are technicians only.

2. **Market > Marketing > Aesthetic > Functionality** — "The product with better marketing wins. Every time." [SSSS:DT.2]

3. **Things will never be clear** — Marketing = math + human behavior. "Even foremost marketing experts have to take their best guess." [SSSS:DT.3]

4. **Can't specify everything, but need a plan** — Agile > waterfall post-launch. "#1 advantage is reaction time." [SSSS:DT.4]

5. **Fail fast and recover** — Bad decisions, wasted money, buggy code. Accept and move on. [SSSS:DT.5]

6. **Never be "done"** — Always features, marketing tests, new markets. "The idea of building an app and sitting back to collect a check is a pipe dream." [SSSS:DT.6]

7. **No instant gratification** — "First month you launch you will be lucky to break $100." Snowball effect takes time. [SSSS:DT.7]

8. **Process is King** — Document repeatable processes. "Without process it's impossible to delegate." [SSSS:DT.8]

9. **Nothing is one-time** — Marketing, dev, support, SEO — all require ongoing effort. [SSSS:DT.9]

## Dollarize Your Time [SSSS:MS.3]

### Approach #1: Freelance Rate
Поиск сравнимых фрилансеров. Минимум $50/час как starting point.

### Approach #2: Salary ÷ 2000
($60K salary + 30% benefits) ÷ 2000 = $39 → round to $40/hr.

### Realizations:

1. **Outsourcing is a bargain** — $6/hr VA vs your $50/hr = 8x leverage
2. **Keep work and play separate** — "Work hard and play hard, but never do both at once"
3. **Wasting time is bad** — Notebook everywhere, capture insights
4. **Information consumption only good when produces something** — Action notes! "A $4.99 audio book is actually a cost if it chews up 6 hours and provides no actionable items."

### Long-term goal: $100/hr dollarized rate.

## Biggest Roadblocks [SSSS:MS.4]

1. **No Market** — Building something nobody wants. Fix: Market First Approach.
2. **Fear** — "The terror wears off pretty quickly." Overcome it → massive confidence boost.
3. **Lack of Goals** — Written goals + public commitment + accountability.
4. **Inconsistency** — Information diet. "Is this activity getting me closer to my launch date?"
5. **Doing Everything Yourself** — "Send the task to my VA. Took 90 seconds. 24 hours later, zip file. Cost: $2." Start small, increase gradually. 20-60 hours saved per month.
