# Scaling & Leverage — $100M Leads (Hormozi)

## [100ML:MBN] More Better New

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section III, Chapter "Core Four On Steroids: More Better New"

**Core Principle:** When you're doing the Core Four but still want MORE engaged leads, there are exactly two boosters you can apply to ANY method — on your own. Do MORE of what works. Do it BETTER. Only then try something NEW. In that order. Always.

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Hit a plateau, want to scale any advertising method, uncertain what to do next
- AVOID WHEN: N/A — this is the universal scaling framework
- COMBINES WITH: All [100ML:WO], [100ML:CF], [100ML:CO], [100ML:PA] — applies to every method

**Key Examples:**
- Agency owner at $2M/year thought he "saturated" the $15.1B chiropractor market with $30K/month on ONE platform. No content, no cold outreach, no other platforms.
- Another attendee: same niche, $30K ad spend across FOUR platforms per WEEK
- Weight loss entrepreneur afraid $40K/month would saturate a platform with 1B+ daily users in a $60B industry

"There are more leads out there than you can possibly imagine."

### [100ML:MBN.1] More — The Rule of 100

**Do more of the advertising that works until it "breaks."**

Even with no improvements, doubling inputs roughly doubles engaged leads. "The biggest increases often come from advertising MORE."

**Rule of 100:** Advertise by doing 100 primary actions every day, for 100 days straight.

Applied to each Core Four:
- **Warm Reach Outs:** 100 reach outs per day (emails, texts, DMs, calls)
- **Post Content:** 100 minutes per day making content. Release at least one piece daily.
- **Cold Reach Outs:** 100 reach outs per day (with automation for scale)
- **Paid Ads:** 100 minutes per day making ads + 100 days straight of running them

"If you do 100 primary actions per day, and you do it for 100 days straight, you will get more engaged leads. Commit to the rule of 100 and you will never go hungry again."

### [100ML:MBN.2] Better — Test One Thing Per Week Per Platform

"Getting better gets you more leads for the same effort."

You do more until it breaks → the drop-off point becomes obvious → fix the constraint → do more again.

**Find the constraint:** The step where the most leads drop off. Example:
- 30% Optin → 5% Apply (CONSTRAINT — biggest drop) → 50% Schedule
- Improving constraint by +5% = 100% more leads (2×)
- Improving other steps by +5% = 10-16% more leads

**Testing protocol (used in every Hormozi company):**
1. Run ONE split test per week per platform
2. Every Monday: look at results, pick winners
3. Write down results in a log of ALL tests (institutional knowledge)
4. Come up with next test to beat current "best"
5. If you can't beat current best in FOUR tries (one month), move to next constraint

"Thousands of tiny tests separate winners from beginners."

### [100ML:MBN.3] New — New Placements, Platforms, Core Four

Only after exhausting More and Better do the real returns come from New.

**The Size Of The Pie Fallacy:** Entrepreneurs mistakenly assume the tiny slice of the universe they advertise to is the ENTIRE available market. "This is why most businesses stay small."

**Order of "New" (most leads per work):**
1. **New placements** on current platform (Instagram stories vs posts vs reels)
2. **New platforms** with familiar placements (YouTube shorts → Instagram reels)
3. **New Core Four activity** (add cold outreach on top of content + ads)

"When to do new: When the returns from more↔better are lower than what you could get from a new placement or new way of advertising."

**Metrics:**
- Input tracking: daily actions per method
- Output tracking: engaged leads per method per day/week
- Constraint identification: step-by-step conversion rates
- Test velocity: tests per week per platform
- Marginal return: additional leads per additional unit of effort

**Reversal:**
- Don't jump to "new" before exhausting "more" and "better" — that's the #1 mistake
- "New" is much harder in practice — exhaust more/better first
- Each "new" channel takes 3-6 months to establish

## [100ML:SC] Scaling — Lead Getters & Leverage

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section IV "Get Lead Getters"

**Core Principle:** A $100M leads machine requires leverage — getting more leads for less of YOUR work. You get leverage by having OTHER people do the Core Four on your behalf. These are "lead getters." Four types: Customers (referrals), Employees, Agencies, Affiliates.

**The Leverage Principle:**
"An old lady can lift a semi-truck with a long enough lever. The strongest man in the world, without one, can't."

**Four Scenarios of Leverage:**
1. YOU are the lead getter → Work: HIGH, Leads: LOW, Leverage: LOW
2. You GET one lead getter → Work: LOW, Leads: LOW, Leverage: HIGH
3. You get LOTS of lead getters → Work: HIGH, Leads: HIGH, Leverage: HIGHER
4. You get a lead getter WHO GETS lead getters → Work: LOW, Leads: HIGH, Leverage: HIGHEST

"Now you've got the makings of a $100M Leads machine."

**Key insight:** Lead getters start as leads themselves. They get interested in your stuff via Core Four, then get OTHER people interested too. Ideally, every lead becomes a lead getter.

## [100ML:RF] Customer Referrals — Word of Mouth

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section IV, Chapter "#1 Customer Referrals"

**Core Principle:** Referrals are the lowest cost, highest profit, and best quality leads. Nothing scales like word of mouth because it's EXPONENTIAL, not linear. One brings two. Two bring four. Four bring eight. "The best source of new work is the work on your desk."

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Always. Product is good enough that customers get results. Every business.
- AVOID WHEN: Never — but fixing product quality comes BEFORE expecting referrals
- COMBINES WITH: All Core Four (referrals make every other method more effective), [100ML:AF] (affiliates are the "other people" version of referrals)

**Key Examples:**
- Gym Launch: ads shut off for 2 weeks unknowingly — still $500K+/week from word of mouth alone
- 700+ gym owner audience: almost the ENTIRE room raised their hand when asked "who learned about us from another gym owner?"
- Dropbox: 39×'d business in 15 months with referral program
- PayPal: referral program → 1M users in 2 years → 100M users in 6 years

### Referral Growth Equation

Referrals (in) - Churned customers (out) = Growth

- Referrals > Churn: you grow WITHOUT any other advertising
- Referrals = Churn: you need advertising to grow
- Referrals < Churn: you must advertise just to break even (most businesses)

**If referral % > churn % → your business compounds every month. This is how companies become $100M+ businesses.**

### Why Most Don't Get Referrals (Two Reasons)

**Reason 1: Product isn't good enough.** "If your product were exceptional, people would already know about it and you'd have more business than you could handle."

**Price - Value = Goodwill.** More goodwill = more word of mouth.

### [100ML:RF.1] Six Ways to Give More Value (Get Referrals Through Product)

1. **Sell Better Customers** — find what best customers have in common, target ONLY those. Better fit = more value = more goodwill. Portfolio company broke years-long plateau by narrowing to 15% of customers who had lowest churn.

2. **Set Better Expectations** — lower promises while maintaining quality = room to overdeliver. Lower until close rates decrease, then stop.

3. **Get More People Better Results** — survey best customers, find common ACTIONS, force all customers to repeat those actions. Gym Launch: if gym owner ran ads + made sale in first 7 days → LTGP tripled. Match guarantee conditions to actions that get best results.

4. **Make Faster Wins** — deliver in smallest increments, give frequent updates, force wins in first 48 hours (lasting impression window). BAMFAM: Book-A-Meeting-From-A-Meeting. Add 50% to timelines → always deliver early.

5. **Keep Making Stuff Better** — monthly cycle: survey → find most common problem → get feedback from customers who overcame it → improve product → test on struggling customers → roll out. Repeat forever.

6. **Tell Them What To Buy Next** — keep selling. More products = more value = more referral opportunities. Neglecting back-end offers → customers fall off → no referrals.

**"One Question to Rule Them All"**: You've lost all customers but one. All advertising is banned. All customers must come from this one customer. How would you treat them? Write it out. Then DO IT.

### [100ML:RF.2] Reason 2: They Never Ask — Seven Ways to Ask for Referrals

1. **One-Sided Referral Benefit** — Pay customer your CAC for a three-way introduction. Or give friend CAC-equivalent discount. Always ask for the spouse (household discount).

2. **Two-Sided Referral Benefits** — Pay BOTH parties (Dropbox/PayPal model). Half CAC to referrer (cash/credit), half to friend (credit/discount). Ex: $100 cash to referrer, $100 off for friend.

3. **Ask Right When They Buy** — On contract/checkout: "Who else would you like to do this with?" HALF of one salesman's sales were referrals just from asking this.

4. **Referrals As Negotiation Chip** — Customer wants discount? Trade for introductions. "I can't do $400, but introduce me to 3 friends right now and I'll cut the initiation fee."

5. **Referral Events** — 1-4 week events: "Bring-a-friend," "Spouse Challenge," "Accountability Buddy." Points/credits/bragging rights for bringing friends.

6. **Ongoing Referral Programs** — Talk about benefits of doing things with others ALL the time — in content, outreach, ads. 33% boost in total sign-ups.

7. **Unlockable Referral Bonuses** — VIP bonuses, courses, merchandise, premium support unlocked by referring + leaving testimonial.

**Killer combo:** Give everyone a gift card for 1/3 cost of program. Expiration in 7-14 days. Customer gives it to friend ("I got this gift card, do you want it?"). Write friend's name on it. OR sell gift cards at 90% off as purchasable gifts → "you get PAID to get new customers."

**Metrics:**
- Referral rate (% of customers who refer per month)
- Churn rate (% of customers who leave per month)
- Referral:Churn ratio (>1.0 = compounding growth)
- CAC of referred vs non-referred customers
- LTGP of referred vs non-referred customers

**Reversal:**
- "Referrals aren't an advertising method you can 'do.' It's a way of doing business."
- Referring is always a RISK for the customer (they risk their goodwill with their friend)
- No amount of referral tactics compensate for a bad product

## [100ML:SC.1] Employees as Lead Getters

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section IV, Chapter "#2 Employees"

**Core Principle:** Employees do the Core Four on your behalf. Trade 40 hours of doing for 4 hours of managing. Then trade 20 hours of managing for 4 hours of leading a manager. End result: 4 hours of work for 200 hours of lead-getting.

**Key Insight:** "You get rich from what you make. You become wealthy from what you own." Business with you doing everything = high-paying job worth ~$0. Business running without you = valuable asset worth $10M+.

**Hiring = Internal Core Four:**
- Warm Outreach → Asking your network
- Cold Outreach → Recruiting
- Post Content → Posting job openings
- Paid Ads → Promoting job postings

**Training: The 3Ds — Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate**
1. **Document** — Make a checklist of exactly how you do it. Record yourself. Test: can you do A+ work ONLY following your own directions?
2. **Demonstrate** — Walk them through checklist step by step. Adjust for confusion points.
3. **Duplicate** — They do it in front of you following the checklist. Fix checklist until right. "If they get it wrong, WE got it wrong."

**Key training principles:**
- If they follow directions exactly but get wrong result → praise for following, fix the checklist
- Reward good stuff, avoid punishment during training
- Give ONE piece of feedback at a time
- Retrain team when performance dips (they stopped doing an important step)

**Returns calculation:** Total Payroll / Total Engaged Leads = Cost per lead. Target: LTGP ≥ 3× all costs per customer.

## [100ML:AG] Agencies

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section IV, Chapter "#3 Agencies"

**Core Principle:** Use agencies to LEARN new methods and platforms, not as permanent outsourcers. The right way: pay them to teach you, then build internal capability. "I paid $6000 for 8 hours that taught me a skill that's made me millions."

**Hormozi's Agency Framework:**
"I want to do what you do in my business, but I don't know how. I'd like to work with you for 6 months so I can learn. I'll pay extra for you to break down why you make decisions. Then I'll train my team. Once they can do it, I'd like to switch to a lower-cost consulting arrangement."

**Two-agency approach:**
1. Hire "good enough" agency to learn platform ropes
2. Hire elite agency to maximize performance
3. Compare your team vs agency until you beat them regularly
4. Cancel and scale what you learned

**Picking an agency (10 criteria):**
1. Referral from someone you know who got results
2. Prominent company case studies
3. Waiting list (demand > supply)
4. Realistic expectations in sales process
5. Long-term strategy focus (no short-term hacks)
6. Clear requirements from you
7. Regular meeting schedule
8. Simple progress tracking
9. Strong offer (value equation)
10. Expensive (good agencies cost money — but not all expensive ones are good)

**Timeline to independence:** Started at ~1 year to match agency → improved to ~6 months.

## [100ML:AF] Affiliates & Partners

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section IV, Chapter "#4 Affiliates and Partners"

**Core Principle:** An affiliate is an independent business that tells THEIR audience to buy YOUR stuff. Each affiliate adds another stream of leads. Affiliates cause crazy scaling because every new affiliate = compounding growth every month.

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Have a proven product, can handle the complexity of managing external partners, want exponential growth
- AVOID WHEN: Unproven product, can't manage employees yet (affiliates are harder), early stage
- COMBINES WITH: [100ML:RF] (treat affiliates like customers), [100ML:SC.1] (employees manage affiliate program), [100ML:LM] (affiliates distribute your lead magnets)

**Key Examples:**
- Prestige Labs: $150K total sales in first 3 weeks → $450K/week by week 4 from 400+ gym affiliates
- ALAN: 1 super-affiliate → 10 agencies/month → 50 businesses/month → 2500 leads/month → $1.7M/month in 6 months
- $75M+ revenue across 5000+ affiliates in Hormozi's businesses

### [100ML:AF.1] Six Steps to Build an Affiliate Army

**Step 1: Find Ideal Affiliates**
Questions about your best customers: What do they buy? (→ who provides it?) Where do they go? (→ nearby businesses?) What do they do? (→ who provides those services?) Where do they work?

Categories: softwares, products, equipment, services, groups, events. Multi-category overlap = high-potential affiliate.

**Step 2: Make Them an Offer**
Affiliates are businesses → offer them a new way to make money. Use standard advertising: call out + value elements + CTA.

Value structure: "Make more money from current customers + get more leads (dream outcome)... high chance of working since customers already want it (perceived likelihood)... without building/delivering/supporting the product (effort)... start selling tomorrow (time delay)."

**Step 3: Qualify Them — Make Them Invest**
Two ways to invest:
1. **Make them a customer** — buy and use the product
2. **Make them an expert** — pay for certification/training (charge 10-20% of what average active affiliate makes in first 12 months)

"If they pay, they'll pay attention."

**Step 4: Figure Out What To Pay Them**

Three-tier payout structure based on maximum allowable CAC:
- **Tier 1 (25% of CAC):** Agrees to initial terms (signs up, buys products)
- **Tier 2 (50% of CAC):** Activates (finishes certification, does launch)
- **Tier 3 (100% of CAC):** Sustains performance (maintains X customers/month)

Hidden benefit: average payout << maximum CAC. Use the difference for contests, acquisition, incentives.

**Step 5: Get Them Advertising — Launch (Whisper-Tease-Shout)**

### [100ML:AF.2] Whisper-Tease-Shout Launch Method

**Whisper** (6+ weeks out): Think "Call Outs." Keep product mysterious, hint at how big a deal it is. Show behind the scenes. Embed curiosity. Every 4-6 weeks → every 2-3 weeks as launch approaches.

**Tease** (30 days out): Think "Elements of Value." Reveal product, make date public, show value elements via What-Who-When framework. Weekly → twice weekly as launch approaches.

**Shout** (3 days out): Think "Call to Action." Specific actions, bonuses, scarcity, urgency. Twice daily → every few hours → every 30 min until launch.

"Good launches have the work done ahead of time. Do ALL the work for affiliates. Then they plug and play."

**Step 6: Keep Them Advertising — Integration**

Three integration strategies (easiest to hardest):
1. **Give away your lead magnet** with every purchase of their stuff (makes their offer more valuable)
2. **Sell your lead magnet** to their audience (affiliate keeps all revenue, you upsell core offer)
3. **Sell your core offer** directly (split revenue)

### Affiliate Economics

Compare cost to GET an affiliate with gross profit from ALL customers they bring.

Example: $4K to get affiliate × affiliate sells $10K/month × 12 months × 75% margins - 40% payout = $54K left / $4K CAC = 12.5:1 ratio.

**Improve ratio:** Lower CAC (better ads/offer), increase activation (launch process), increase LTGP (integration process).

**Metrics:**
- Cost per affiliate acquisition
- Affiliate activation rate (% who actually start selling)
- Revenue per active affiliate per month
- Affiliate retention rate
- LTGP:CAC ratio per affiliate (vs per customer)
- Tier distribution (% at each payout tier)

## The Roadmap — Zero to $100M

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section V, Chapter "The Roadmap"

### [100ML:SC.2] Six Levels of Advertising Scale

**Level 1:** Your friends know about the stuff you sell.
→ Primary action: Warm outreach. One offer, one avatar, one platform.

**Level 2:** You consistently let everyone you know about your stuff.
→ Primary actions: Maximize warm outreach + start posting content consistently.

**Level 3:** You get employees to help do more advertising.
→ Hire people to advertise profitably on your behalf.

**Level 4:** Your product is good enough to get consistent referrals (25%+).
→ Focus on product quality until consistent referrals, then scale advertising with bigger team. "This is where most people mess up. They let their product slip and never recover."

**Level 5:** You advertise in more places in more ways with more people.
→ Multiple platforms, multiple methods, expand team for each new channel.

**Level 6:** You hire killers.
→ Experienced executives run advertising departments without you. Stronger incentives. "This is how we crossed $100M+ in portfolio revenue and beyond."

### [100ML:SC.3] Open To Goal

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section V, Chapter "Advertising in Real Life"

Rule of 100... but for the big kids. Don't commit to a number of ACTIONS — commit to the work until you hit a specific number of OUTCOMES. No matter what.

Example: Gym chain — sales managers set own schedules but must sign up 5 new members per day. Done by lunch? Leave early. Takes 18 hours? So be it.

Hormozi's flyer lesson: put out 300 flyers, got 0 leads. Mentor tests with 5,000, then does 5,000/day for a month when winner found. "I promised myself I would NEVER let effort be the reason ANYTHING didn't work for me."

"Most people dramatically underestimate the volume it takes to make advertising work. They're doing 1/1500th the level of effort required."

### One-Page Advertising Checklist

1. Pick type of engaged lead: Customers, Affiliates, Employees, or Agencies
2. Pick Rule of 100 or Open to Goal. Commit to daily actions.
3. Fill out advertising checklist for daily action (what, who, where, how much, offer)
4. Do daily action until you can afford to pay someone else
5. When you can, go back to step 1: make Employees the new target. Repeat.

**Hormozi's 3 ROI Habits:**
1. Wake early (4-5 AM) — actually means going to bed early
2. Get right to work — no rituals, coffee and go
3. No meetings until noon — fully focused work block

"If the number terrifies you, you're doing it right. Trust the data. This is how you scale. And that's why most people never do."
