## Core Principle: Stair Step Method of Entrepreneurship [SP:GR.1]

Прогрессия от простых продуктов к standalone SaaS:

**Step 1 — First Product:** Простой продукт (WordPress plugin, Shopify add-on), один канал трафика. Цель: начать генерировать revenue, изучить основы.

**Step 2 — Rinse & Repeat:** Повторить успех Step 1. Построить portfolio. Освоить free/low-cost traffic channels. Выкупить своё время.

**Step 3 — Standalone SaaS:** Полноценный SaaS. Сложный в build и market. Длинный on-ramp к substantial revenue. Требует experience, skills, confidence из Steps 1-2.

**Decision:** Можно прыгнуть сразу на Step 3, но Stair Step снижает risk значительно.

## Marketing Funnels [SP:MF]

### High-Touch Funnel [SP:MF.1]

```
Awareness → Lead → Opportunity → Demo → Customer → Customer Success
```

- Minimum: $500/мес ACV
- Focus: outbound (cold-calling, cold-email, LinkedIn)
- Close rate benchmark: 25-35% demo-to-customer (Drip's number)
- Suits: Complex products, enterprise clients

### Low-Touch Funnel [SP:MF.2]

```
Traffic → Website → Trial → (Auto) Conversion → Customer → Upgrade/Refer
```

- Suits: Wide market, low price ($10-100/мес)
- Focus: inbound (SEO, PPC, content)
- Lower conversion rates, higher churn, но lower CAC
- Volume game

### SaaS Cheat Code: Dual Funnel [SP:DF.1]

Одновременно:
- Wide low-touch funnel (brand building, volume)
- Premium high-touch funnel (enterprise revenue)

**Пример — SignWell:** Low-touch для professionals/SMB (few documents), High-touch для mortgage brokers (5K-10K documents/month).

Динамика: Low-touch drives early revenue → brand builds → high-touch grows as % of revenue → accelerated growth.

## Big 5 SaaS Marketing Approaches [SP:MA.1]

### 1. SEO [SP:MA.1a]
Не только Google. YouTube (#2), Amazon (top 5), iOS App Store, WordPress Plugin Repository, Shopify App Store, Chrome Web Store.

**Пример — Drip:** Строил WordPress plugins для ranking по email marketing terms в WP Plugin Repository.

### 2. PPC Advertising [SP:MA.1b]
Google AdWords, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Amazon, Capterra, LinkedIn. Tried-and-true, обычно дорого. Быстрые результаты.

### 3. Cold Outreach [SP:MA.1c]
Email, phone, LinkedIn, DMs. Работает лучше когда есть signal (timing).

**Пример — Drip:** Знали что Infusionsoft customers ищут альтернативы при окончании годового контракта → ping через 8 месяцев.

### 4. Integration Marketing [SP:MA.1d]
Единственный approach, который также улучшает продукт. Partner integration → mutual promotion.

**Promotion checklist:**
- Blog post обеих сторон
- Email обоим спискам
- Tweet / social media post
- Knowledge base article
- In-app mention
- Webinar
- Add-on marketplace listing

**Подход:** MVP integration → launch + promote → measure → iterate best performers.

### 5. Content Marketing [SP:MA.1e]
Blog posts, ebooks, audio (podcasts), video (YouTube), courses. Building audience slowly. SEO synergy.

## Three Factor Framework для Выбора Каналов [SP:MA.3]

| Factor | Вопрос | Implications |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Speed** | Как быстро результаты? | Early stage → faster approaches critical |
| **Cost** | Сколько стоит? ($+time) | Higher ACV → больше можно инвестировать |
| **Scalability** | Можно масштабировать? | One-time (ProductHunt) vs ongoing (SEO) |

### Marketing by Price Point [SP:MA.4]:

**ARPA < $50/мес:**
- SEO, content, free tools
- Virality, launch sites
- Low-cost PPC

**ARPA $50-500/мес:**
+ Integration marketing
+ Affiliate marketing
+ Hangouts (forums, Slack groups)
+ Other people's audiences (guest posts, podcasts)

**ARPA $500-5000/мес:**
+ Cold outreach
+ In-person events
+ Display ads
+ PR

**ARPA $5000+/мес:**
+ Enterprise sales team
+ Speaking engagements
+ Everything available

### ICE Framework для Приоритизации [SP:MA.5]

| Metric | Вопрос | Scale |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **I**mpact | Если сработает, какой потенциальный impact? | 1-10 |
| **C**onfidence | Насколько вероятно сработает? | 1-10 |
| **E**ase | Насколько легко выполнить? | 1-10 |

Score = I x C x E (exponential) или (I+C+E)/3 (average).

**Best practice:** 
1. Ранжировать high-level approaches (SEO, PPC, content)
2. Для top approaches — breakout на tactics
3. Tackle highest-rated first
4. Один быстрый + один медленный канал одновременно

## Marketing Experiment Rules [SP:MA.6]

1. **Marketing Changelog** — записывать ВСЁ что пробуете, включая мелочи (copy change on website)
2. **Measure** — отслеживать cost ($ + time) и results для каждого канала
3. **Don't Try Too Much** — "I've seen SaaS companies grow to 7-8 figures by picking just one or two marketing channels and scaling them well"
4. **Double down** — работающий канал → увеличить investment, не распылять
5. **Talk to non-competitors** — чужие founders в том же market могут поделиться что работает

## Word of Mouth — Warning [SP:MA.7]

"Whenever I ask an entrepreneur where most of their leads are coming from and they say word of mouth, I like to keep digging. Usually, the real answer is that they don't know where customers are coming from."

Drip data: WOM = 15-25% new users. Остальное = measurable channels.

**Обязательно:** Attribution! Ask "How did you hear about us?" + cookie-based referral tracking (Ruben Gamez approach: store referral source + landing page в cookie → save to DB при signup → send to Stripe/ChartMogul).

## Funnel Diagnostics [SP:MF.3]

**Fix BOTTOM-UP:**

```
Customers churning fast? → Onboarding or PMF problem
  ↑
Trials not converting to paid? → Onboarding or sales process problem  
  ↑
Website visitors not signing up? → Value proposition or positioning problem
  ↑
Traffic not coming? → Marketing channel problem
```

"If your customers are frustrated and churning out after a few months, it doesn't matter how many people you're sending to your website. Everything at the top of the funnel is a vanity metric when the bottom of your funnel isn't healthy."

## Team Building [SP:TM]

### Departments в SaaS Company [SP:TM.1]:
Product, Design, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Customer Support, Customer Success, HR, Legal, Finance.

### First Hires (typical order) [SP:TM.2]:
1. **Support** — repetitive, lower value than other founder tasks
2. **Sales / Marketing Implementation / Developer** — зависит от skill set founders
3. **Customer Success Manager** — $20-40K MRR range
4. **Product Manager** — >$1M ARR

### Role Combinations [SP:TM.3]:

| Combo | Why It Works |
|-------|-------------|
| Support + Customer Success | Similar skills, customer-facing |
| Dev + DevOps | Technical overlap |
| Marketing + Content | Creative + strategic |
| Sales + Customer Success | For warm inbound leads |

**Warning:** Founder is often 2-3x more effective → task that takes you 10h/week may take hire 20h/week.

### Delegate Roles, Not Tasks [SP:TM.4]

"When building your team you should delegate roles, not tasks."

### Thinker Levels [SP:TM.5]:
- **Task-level** — фокус на текущей задаче
- **Project-level** — жонглирует priorities, смотрит на weeks/months
- **Owner-level** — думает о process improvement, strategic shifts, months/years

At $20-40K MRR: нужны project-level и owner-level thinkers.

## Moats — Защита от Конкурентов [SP:MO]

### 1. Integrations (Network Effect) [SP:MO.1]
Каждая интеграция = причина не уходить. Zapier — 3000+ apps + non-public APIs.

### 2. Strong Brand [SP:MO.2]
"Your brand is your reputation — it's what people say about your company when you're not around."

### 3. Owned Traffic Channels [SP:MO.3]
SEO dominance, 500K unique visitors/month. Dicey moat — algorithms change.

### 4. High Switching Costs [SP:MO.4]
APIs (Stripe, Twilio), tools with data history (Slack), buy-in required (enterprise).

### False Moat: Unique Features [SP:MO.5]
"Features are a fantastic differentiator — for a few months, until a competitor duplicates them." Hamster wheel, не moat.

## Bootstrapping vs Funding [SP:GR.2]

**Superpowers of bootstrapping:**
1. Не нужно разрешение
2. Бизнес не умирает пока не quit (bootstrappers don't run out of money, they run out of motivation)
3. 0.01% chance of $100M (VC) vs 20-30% chance of hundreds of thousands (bootstrap)
4. >99% companies seeking funding don't receive it

**SaaS exit multiples:** 4-8x annual revenue. Каждый $1K MRR = $60K enterprise value (at 5x).

**Mostly bootstrapped:** $100K-$500K funding, run as if bootstrapped. Пример: Customer.io ($250K первый round), Castos ($120K TinySeed).

## Mindset [SP:MS]

### Focus Time [SP:MS.2]
Technical founders: переходить от Maker's Schedule (long blocks) к Manager's Schedule (1-hour increments). Нанимать developers, учиться marketing/sales.

### Burnout Prevention [SP:MS.3]
"For bootstrappers, a lack of money isn't what kills businesses. It's founder burnout." → Hire support person первым делом.

### Cofounder [SP:MS.4]
56% успешных indie SaaS — solo founders. Если берёшь cofounder: complementary skills, vesting (4 years), lawyer. "Will joining forces make the company more valuable?"

## SaaS Cheat Code: Virality [SP:VR]

### Strong Viral Loop [SP:VR.1]
Product value = connecting people. Slack, SavvyCal, SignWell.

### Weak Viral Loop [SP:VR.2]
"Powered by X" badges, watermarks on free plans. Less impact, less targeted.

### Building Virality [SP:VR.3]
"Extremely difficult to retrofit virality into your product." Start from the beginning. But: "you can absolutely build a SaaS company to millions in revenue with a zero viral coefficient."
