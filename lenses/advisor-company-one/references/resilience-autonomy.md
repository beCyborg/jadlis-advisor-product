## Resilience — 3 Learnable Traits `[CO1:RA.2]`

Source: Dean Becker, Adaptiv Learning Systems (research since 1997)

> "The level of resilience determines success far more than education, training, or experience."

### Trait 1: Acceptance of Reality

Не wishful thinking, а down-to-earth view. "Большая часть того, что происходит — вне нашего контроля. Лучшее что можем — слегка рулить лодкой."

**Example:** Danielle LaPorte — уволена из собственной компании → yoga, tears, friends → regrouped → new website за недели → consulting waiting list → bestselling book.

**Dark humor as tool:** Жена Jarvis — firefighter. Юмор = coping mechanism, который fire chief поощряет. Не обесценивание, а добавление света в тёмные ситуации.

### Trait 2: Sense of Purpose

Мотивация meaning > money. Purpose makes stress worth it. Стресс от запуска нового продукта, который aligned с purpose → worth it. Стресс от бессмысленного роста → destructive.

### Trait 3: Ability to Adapt

- Canada: 42% jobs at risk from automation (Ryerson)
- USA: 62% jobs in danger within 10-20 years (White House, 2016)
- Company of one advantage: креативное решение проблем ТРУДНО автоматизировать
- Jarvis web design: recession → lower prices than agencies but same quality → wins both ways

**Actionable:** Каждый квартал ask: "Что изменилось в моём рынке? Как я адаптируюсь?"

## Autonomy — Master First, Then Control `[CO1:RA.3]`

### The Autonomy Ladder

1. **Sponge phase** — учись всё о profession, industry, customers
2. **Skill mastery** — стань in-demand в своём skillset
3. **Selective autonomy** — выбирай клиентов/проекты (waiting list)
4. **Full control** — business around YOUR life

**Kaitlin Maud Example:**
- 5 лет в agency → building skills + network
- Side income от freelance → stable enough to leave
- 8 hours agency work = 4 hours freelance (efficiency gain)
- Clients: Beats by Dre, Taco Bell, Adobe, Toms
- Peak productivity 9AM-1PM → no meetings in that window

**Sol Orwell / Examine.com:**
- 7 figures/year, no VC, no desire to sell
- "Freedom to not fill every minute with work"
- Long midday breaks for dog walks, dance classes on Wednesday afternoons

### ROWE Model (Results-Only Work Environment)
- No set schedules, all meetings optional
- Employees sculpt job however they want
- Results: productivity UP, satisfaction UP, turnover DOWN (Ressler & Thompson, 10+ years research)

## Purpose Over Passion `[CO1:RA.5]`

### Why "Follow Your Passion" Is Bad Advice

**University of Quebec (Vallerand, 2003):** Students passionate about sports, arts, music — only 3% of jobs exist in those areas.

**Cal Newport:** Passion is SIDE EFFECT of mastery, not prerequisite. "Craftsperson mindset" (what can I offer the world?) > "passion mindset" (what can the world offer me?).

**Barbara Corcoran (Shark Tank):** Didn't follow passion — discovered it through hard work. Focus on problem-solving > passion-chasing.

### Two Prerequisites for Successful Leap

1. **Skills in demand** — skilled at something BEFORE leaping (not after)
2. **Tested with small jump** — proved viability before full commitment

**Jarvis personal example:**
- Web design success: left agency when clients wanted to follow him → safe runway  <!-- privacy-ok: термин из книжного конспекта, не финансы владельца -->
- Online courses: years of transition, waited until revenue proved viability
- FAILED: business consulting in 20s (no skills), two software companies (no market validation)

### Purpose ≠ Passion Framework

| Aspect | Purpose | Passion |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Based on | Core values, consistent | Whims, changeable |
| Drives | Long-term sustainability | Short-term excitement |
| Test | "Would I do this even if hard?" | "Do I feel excited right now?" |
| Result | Profit + meaning + retention | Potential burnout + pivot |

**Examples of purpose-driven businesses:**
- **Patagonia:** Environmental stewardship → "Don't buy this jacket" ads → top 5 in 1% for Planet → record sales during 2008 recession
- **Seventh Generation:** Impact on next 7 generations → B-corp → $250M revenue → acquired by Unilever
- **CVS:** Stopped selling tobacco ($billions revenue) → aligned with health purpose

### Kotter & Heskett Research
Purpose-based companies outperform in stock price by 12x factor. Without purpose: harder to rally employees, harder for customers to connect.

## Anti-Hustle Mindset `[CO1:RA.6]`

### Workaholism Is Not a Strategy

Wayne Oates (coined "workaholism", 1971): hustlers DON'T outperform non-hustlers. Only measurable impact: higher stress, more work-life conflict, deteriorating health. NO relationship between workaholism and financial reward.

**David Heinemeier Hansson (Basecamp):**
- Pressure to work more doesn't just pass down — it's AMPLIFIED through company
- Encourage: life outside work, sleep/recuperation, calmer work habits

### Productivity Science

| Finding | Source | Implication |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Multitasking reduces productivity 40% | Microsoft Research | Single-task, don't multi-task |
| Email interruptions reduce IQ 10 points (2x marijuana) | Hewlett-Packard | Batch communication |
| 23 min 15 sec to recover from each interruption | Gloria Mark, UC Irvine | Protect deep work blocks |
| Productivity drops drastically after 55 hours/week | John Pencavel, Stanford | Work less, not more |
| Bad decisions when time-strapped and too busy | Mullainathan & Shafir | Leave unplanned time |

### Jarvis Personal System
- Months off from interviews/calls/meetings for creation
- Mondays & Fridays: no communication (writing/coding)
- Thursdays: meetings day
- Rarely >1 hour weekends
- Productivity audit 1-2x/year: track tasks, time, distractions for 1-2 weeks

### Basecamp Anti-Interruption Rules
- No calendar sharing (prevents "you have free time" abuse)
- 8 hours/day of UNINTERRUPTED work expected
- Company demands employee time rarely, must ASK for it
- No expectation of immediate response (except emergencies)
- 4-day workweek in summer (Fridays off)

## Relationships as Business Foundation `[CO1:RA.7]`

### Chris Brogan Model

- DON'T sell, BUILD relationships first
- Promote friends and interesting people WITHOUT being asked
- "Who do I know who could benefit from connecting with this person?"
- Selling becomes natural when trust is built through genuine connection

### Company vs Audience

> "You can't OWN an audience" — Chris Brogan

Implied ownership → temptation to over-sell → audience turns against you.
Use mailing list mostly for VALUE (articles, news), occasionally for sales.

### Personal Touch at Scale

| Company Size | Advantage |
|-------------|-----------|
| Solo/Small | Know customers by name, need, motivation |
| | Read and personally reply to newsletter responses |
| | "How are you, Paul Jarvis?" > "How are you, Cleveland?" |
| Large trying to be small | Anheuser-Busch: acquired 10 craft beer brands |
| | Staples: "Summon Your Inner Pro" B2B campaign |

## Lifestyle Design Checklist

Decision framework для каждого бизнес-решения:

1. **Life first:** "What type of life do I want? How do I want to spend my days?" THEN work backwards to business model. (James Clear approach) `[CO1:RA.8]`

2. **Rules test (James Clear):**
   - Rule 1: Products must take little to no management (pre-recorded, no live sessions)
   - Rule 2: One-time fees, no retainers (keynote: fly in, talk, leave)

3. **"Enough" number** — определена ли точка достаточности? `[CO1:SB.2]`

4. **Overhead audit** — "OVERHEAD = DEATH". Можно ли убрать расходы? `[CO1:SS.1]`

5. **Autonomy check** — работа вращается вокруг МОЕЙ жизни, или я вращаюсь вокруг работы?

6. **Purpose alignment** — это решение поддерживает мой purpose?

7. **Customer success** — мои клиенты становятся успешнее благодаря мне?

## The Pointe Restaurant Model `[CO1:RA.9]`

Tofino, award-winning dining. Personal touches cost almost nothing extra but create luxury brand:
- Champagne greeting
- Waitstaff gets to know you personally
- Chef appears to check in (not to upsell)
- Car brought around after dinner
- Result: huge premium pricing + word of mouth

**Lesson:** Personal touches + quality >> marketing spend. Works at any scale, especially small.
