# The Embedded Entrepreneur: Build in Public — Transparency, Feedback Loops, Trust, Community

Source: "The Embedded Entrepreneur" by Arvid Kahl (2021), Part V (Audience-Building)

---

## Audience-Building Philosophy `[EE:BIP]`

### Core Principle `[EE:BIP.1]`

Audience-building — не просто "собери followers и продай им что-то". Это построение двух параллельных брендов:
1. **Personal brand** — ты как domain expert, carries beyond any single business
2. **Business brand** — продукт, решение, value proposition

**Two Goals**:
1. Become a trusted domain expert in your community
2. Build a product that your audience needs

**Audience-building never ends.** Это ongoing process, producing more value the longer you stick with it.

### 3 Pillars of Growth `[EE:BIP.2]`

1. **Engagement** — talk WITH people, not AT them. Works from day one `[EE:EN]`
2. **Empowerment** — lift people up, multiply their visibility `[EE:EM]`
3. **Valuable Content** — provide meaningful, regular content `[EE:VC]`

**Build in Public loop**: Learn from community → Validate problem → Build solution → Release → Observe reactions → Learn again

### Abundance Mindset `[EE:BIP.3]`

- Community is NOT zero-sum game. Sharing attention = triple win (you, followers, amplified person)
- Competition not a problem in community context
- Amplifying others' messages = building relationships, not losing followers
- Extending to relationships: conversations, not just sales opportunities

## Engagement `[EE:EN]`

### Audience Audition `[EE:EN.2]`

**Core Concept**: Put yourself in front of someone ELSE's established audience and attract followers from there.

**Two phases**:
1. **Hunt**: Find engagement opportunities with large accounts
2. **Gather**: Engage with original author AND their responding followers

**Finding Audition Candidates**:
- Accounts that trigger regular engagement
- Sizable following that doesn't overwhelm them
- Active, positive, supportive followers
- Eager to invite new voices

**Setup**:
1. Top 5 accounts → Private Twitter List "Audience Audition"
2. TweetDeck → add column for list → turn on notifications
3. Be among FIRST replies (Gestalt law of proximity: closeness = perceived similarity)

### Rules of Successful Engagement `[EE:EN.3]`

**Avatar, Bio, Pinned Tweet** — your Twitter profile = invitation to become a friend:
- **Avatar**: Real photo, friendly, use for long time (changing confuses people)
- **Handle**: Full name preferred. Pseudonyms = trust barrier
- **Bio**: Share what you talk about + things you are + current projects. Make people curious
- **Pinned tweet**: Best-performing or most recent project. Don't let it go stale
- **Link**: Personal website or link page (linktree/bio.fm)

### Four Engagement Actions `[EE:EN.4]`

Every reply should do at least one:

1. **Expand** — zoom out, bring broader insight, perspective shift from another field
2. **Focus** — zoom in, find underlying motivations, follow-up questions → trigger direct interaction with author
3. **Syndicate** — expose conversation to bigger audience via quote-tweet (not just retweet — own spin + own metrics)
4. **Invite** — bring specific expert into conversation → creates connections in multiple directions

### Good Questions `[EE:EN.5]`

Bad: "Why is that?" (100% demanding, zero value)
Good: "I experienced similar in my job as Y. Now with Z audience, I don't see it. Why do you think that could be?" (intent + context + perspective shift)

### What to Avoid `[EE:EN.6]`

- **Don't be needy** — never ask for follows
- **Don't spam** — don't be first reply on ALL their tweets ("reply guy" = shunned)
- **Don't condescend** — keep back retorts, "but actually's". Be kind + interesting
- **Don't dismiss/trash-talk** — disagreement is fine, "You're wrong" is attack. Use "Thank you for that perspective, here is what I am thinking..."
- **Don't beg for engagement** — desperate = signals low value

### Conversation Types Worth Engaging `[EE:EN.7]`

**Engage**: Help requests, moral support (listen first!), alternative viewpoints, refining vague ideas, sharing journeys
**Skip**: Fishing for compliments, pity without way out, unreflected cognitive biases, baiting/trolling

## Empowerment `[EE:EM]`

### Amplification as Empowerment `[EE:EM.1]`

**Ways to empower (cost: seconds)**:
- Amplify message → share with larger/different audience
- Endorse message → stamp of approval
- Congratulate success → boost confidence
- Console through failure → boost confidence
- Invite as expert → cement reputation
- Call out positive impact → cement reputation

**Chain example**: Someone asks question → you retweet → 3 experts answer
Result: You = facilitator of reputation + enabler of success + promoter of community

### Opportunity Surface `[EE:EM.2]`

**Concept**: "Lucky" opportunities are serendipitous but can be INVITED by increasing surface area for random positive events.

**How to increase**: Every helpful action = charging the "luck anticipation battery". Trust accumulates. When you need support, it's released.

**FeedbackPanda book launch example**: 8 months of free value → 4,000 followers → launch day → immediate retweets, purchases, selfies with book → 350+ copies in 24 hours. "People were waiting to reciprocate."

### Making Friends Has a Place in Business `[EE:EM.3]`

Stop seeing Twitter as "content distribution channel" → start seeing as "community of peers"
- Selfish in a way that benefits others = win-win
- Making friends, not just followers
- Honest relationship > shallow connection

### Reciprocation `[EE:EM.4]`

Whenever you help selflessly → others notice → tiny amount of trust → accumulates → "tension" builds → released when you launch/need support.

**FeedbackPanda newsletter strategy**: Each week highlighted one customer (photo + backstory) → customers assigned brand of "empathetic and selfless company" → became part of community, not just business.

## Valuable Content `[EE:VC]`

### Definition `[EE:VC.1]`

Content = "information made available." Not just articles. A quote, a question, a conversation, a screenshot — all content if someone gains value.

Value = form of gain (monetary, peace of mind, enjoyment, stimulation, connection).

### Content Types `[EE:VC.2]`

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Insight** `[EE:VC.3]` | Short, quotable wisdom | Expertise signaling |
| **Thread** `[EE:VC.4]` | Multi-tweet essay or curated list | Deep engagement, suspense |
| **Quote** `[EE:VC.5]` | External wisdom with attribution | Easy value, curation |
| **Observation** `[EE:VC.6]` | Quote-tweet with commentary | Double value pack |
| **Question** `[EE:VC.7]` | Invite followers to share knowledge | Community learning |
| **Link** `[EE:VC.8]` | External resource with summary | Curation, SEO |
| **Progress Update** `[EE:VC.9]` | BIP milestone, celebration, failure | Authenticity, trust |
| **Visualization** `[EE:VC.10]` | Charts, mind-maps, iconography | Eye-catching, accessibility |
| **Shoutout** `[EE:VC.11]` | Highlight community member | Empowerment, connections |
| **Follower List** `[EE:VC.12]` | Recommend accounts to follow | Community-building |
| **Report** `[EE:VC.13]` | Event summary with takeaways | Archiving, bridging |
| **Poll** `[EE:VC.14]` | Vote on topic → discussion | Massive engagement |
| **Meme** `[EE:VC.15]` | Humor related to niche | Ice-breaking |

### Thread Craft `[EE:VC.4]`

- **First tweet = anchor**: Captivating, promise, surprise → they read the whole thing
- **Last tweet = launchpad**: Links, CTAs, newsletter signup → they just loaded on value, ready to reciprocate
- **Pace between**: Suspense or consistently high quality
- **One idea per tweet**: Readable, individually quotable/shareable
- Curated threads (resource lists): Don't overwhelm. Interject personal commentary between links

### Content Accessibility and Reuse `[EE:VC.16]`

**Flywheel Effect**: Blog post → newsletter → podcast episode → cross-link all three
Benefits: Different preferences, diversified audience, SEO from multiple platforms

**Re-using content is NOT a sin**: Accessibility (different formats for different abilities) + Timing (different people need it at different stages) = legitimate reasons to re-share.

**Content Timing**: Repeat content months later. Different people at different stages will resonate differently. "You spoke too soon" is a timing problem, not quality problem.

**Audience Graduation** `[EE:AG]`: Initial audience outgrows your content → either find fresh audience or evolve content for existing audience's new needs. "Champagne problem" — indicates success.

### Avoiding Content Overflow `[EE:VC.17]`

- Check average tweet volume of notable community members → aim for similar or less
- Be a team player, don't hog the activity stream
- Creation vs Curation: Start with curation (finding + presenting others' work) → graduate to creation

## Practice of Audience-Building `[EE:BIP.4]`

### Audience-Building Schedule

Set up systems: Audience-Building Schedule (what to do when) + Accountability mechanism (newsletter subscribers, mastermind group, pacts).

Arvid's system: Started Bootstrapped Founder newsletter → 6 real subscribers → "Suddenly I wasn't writing for a nebulous audience but for real people" → never missed a week.

### Consistency > Perfection `[EE:BIP.5]`

- **Audience growth is geometric, not linear**: 1→100 takes same time as 100→1,000 (network effects)
- **Advertisement Rule of 7**: Customer becomes aware only after 7 exposures. Consistency = showing up on radars
- **Valleys and mountains**: Some days nothing happens → then picked up by large account → wild ride → settle slightly above before. Trust the systems

### Belief Capital `[EE:BIP.6]`

Every positive interaction = expression of someone's belief in your mission. Accumulates over time, grows with audience.

**Celebrate victories** (small and big). Take screenshots and snapshots. Combat recency bias with visible progress.

**Don't measure follower numbers exclusively**: "What gets measured can become a goal." Inflating followers without real relationships = more harm than help. Success is multivariate.

### Impostor Syndrome `[EE:BIP.7]`

Everyone feels it. Embrace the fear, listen to it, then ignore it. "Real impostors don't suffer from impostor syndrome."

Building in public = building capital/wealth. Struggle and journey mean something. People follow you because of vulnerability, not despite it.

**You = Hero's Journey**: Your unique backstory = unfair advantage no one can copy. "Productize yourself" (Naval). Be honest, truthful, quirky, imperfect self.
