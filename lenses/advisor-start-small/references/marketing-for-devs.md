## Core Principle: Email List > Everything

"A mailing list is the most effective marketing tool you will possess. It works in any market. It's a marketing requirement for startups." [SSSS:MK.1]

Targeted mailing list converts at 5-10x higher rate than standard website traffic. If standard converts at 1%, email list converts at 5-10%.

## Two Tiers of Traffic [SSSS:MK.2]

### Top Shelf — Strategies That Will Sustain a Business:

1. **A Mailing List** [SSSS:EM]
2. **A Blog, Podcast or Video Blog** [SSSS:MK.3]
3. **Organic Search (SEO)** [SSSS:SEO]

### Second Shelf — Supplemental:
Social media, PPC, forums, press releases, guest blogging, affiliate programs, banner ads, etc.

**Warning:** "Everything you've heard about marketing on Twitter and Facebook is true, but with a few minor details left out. Such as the fact that the companies who have used Twitter to turn a profit have tens of thousands of subscribers which they've built up over hundreds of hours."

**Decision:** Social media = fun diversion. Real business = Top Shelf strategies.

## Sales Website Design [SSSS:SW]

### Sales Funnel [SSSS:SW.1]

```
See URL → Click → Become Visitor → Become Prospect (email) → Become Buyer
```

At each step you lose the majority. Chance of someone going from URL to buyer on first visit: "almost zero."

### The #1 Goal of Your Website [SSSS:SW.2]

**Turning browsers into prospects (email capture), NOT selling on first visit.**

"Your number one goal, even beyond selling your product, is turning browsers into prospects."

Для получения email нужно 3 вещи:
1. **Trust** — не будешь спамить, не продашь адрес
2. **Relevance** — продукт и emails будут релевантны
3. **Reward** — конкурс, email course, whitepaper, webinar

**Пример — DotNetInvoice:** Форма "получать обновления" = 3 подписки за 8 месяцев. Переключение на "выиграй бесплатную копию" = 30 подписок за 1 месяц.

### 7 Rules of Sales Site Design [SSSS:SW.3]

1. **Home page ≠ most visited page** — Deep linking + search engines send traffic everywhere
2. **Every page needs a call to action** — Email capture, demo, or purchase на КАЖДОЙ странице
3. **Every page needs a single focus** — Ruthlessly eliminate extraneous functionality
4. **Everything within 2 clicks** — Demo, buy, or email capture from any page
5. **Accommodate different reading patterns** — Headlines + bullets для skimmers, short paragraphs для readers
6. **Make buttons look like buttons** — "So clickable people can't help but click them"
7. **No One Reads** — "Text is a terrible selling tool; audio, video and images are always better"

### 5 Core Pages [SSSS:SW.4]

1. **Home** — Single goal: convince visitor to click 1 link. 40-60% abandonment rate на неоптимизированных home pages. Solution: simple page, few options, large buttons.

2. **Tour** — Medium screenshots with data + 1-minute screencasts per page. Minimal text.

3. **Testimonials** — "Do not launch without testimonials." Beta testers, friends, Google Alerts for mentions. Most important at top.

4. **Contact Us** — Contact form + email + toll-free number (voicemail OK) + physical address (use Earth Class Mail ~$20/мес).

5. **Pricing & Purchase** — Far right in navigation. Highlight "best deal" tier. FAQ about payment at bottom. Include upsell. Money-back guarantee.

### Finding Your Hook [SSSS:SW.5]

Hook = 4-second sales pitch = headline of home page.

**3 подхода:**
1. **What + For Whom:** "Simple Proposal Software Made for Designers" (Bidsketch)
2. **Promise/Benefit:** "Save Time. Get Paid Faster" (DotNetInvoice)
3. **Remarkable Feature:** "One Thousand Songs in Your Pocket" (iPod)

## Conversion Rate Optimization [SSSS:SW.6]

**Ключевой инсайт:** Increasing conversion rate > increasing traffic.

```
1,000 visitors × 1% conversion = 10 sales

To add 1 more sale:
  Option A: 100 more visitors (hard)
  Option B: 0.1% conversion increase (easier, higher ROI)
```

**Traffic quality matters more than quantity.** [SSSS:MK.4]

"High quality traffic means each visitor is very close to your ideal customer and they know and trust you."

TechCrunch traffic = low quality (untargeted, no relationship).
Email list traffic = high quality (targeted, trust built over time).

## Email Marketing — Complete Framework [SSSS:EM]

### Building Your List [SSSS:EM.1]

**Strategy #1: Give Something Away**
- PDF report (5-15 pages) on topic your audience is ravenous for
- Пример: Freckle Time Tracking — credit card tutorial PDF
- Пример: JustBeachTowels — "Top 10 Expert Tips for Saving Money on Beach Trip"
- Outsource writing to Elance if needed

**Strategy #2: Offer Email Course**
- 5-day email course (looks hokey, works great)
- Technical audiences prefer report/whitepaper
- Non-technical prefer email courses
- **Test both:** Report for 1 month → course for 1 month → compare

### Autoresponder Series [SSSS:EM.2]

**Key framework:** Instead of broadcast emails, build autoresponder series with 2-4 week gaps.

```
Person #1 signs up today → Email 1 → (2 weeks) → Email 2 → ...
Person #500 signs up in 1 year → Same Email 1 → (2 weeks) → Same Email 2 → ...
```

**Benefit:** Content written once, reused for years. No "throwaway" early emails.

Time-sensitive info (launch announcements) → one-time broadcast separately.

### Email Content Types [SSSS:EM.3]

**Type 1: Relevant Information** (vertical niches)
- Updates every 2-4 weeks
- Monitor: Google Alerts, blogs, YouTube
- Share links + commentary
- Post to blog too (SEO benefit)

**Type 2: Product Updates** (horizontal markets)
- Launch announcements
- Feature surveys
- Less frequent, lower engagement but still valuable

**Content Ideas:**
1. Current events monitoring → share with commentary
2. Q&A from customers/prospects
3. Short interviews with customers/bloggers
4. Hire writer from Elance ($20-30 per article)

### 7 Email Tips [SSSS:EM.4]

1. **Time of Day:** Tue/Wed/Thu, 7am-10:30am (test your audience)
2. **HTML vs Text:** Always send text. HTML for design/marketing audiences. Test.
3. **Never send attachments** — Upload to server, send link
4. **From name matters** — "[Product] Newsletter" not "Admin"
5. **Subject = Headline** — 7 words or less. Ask question, partial statement, use first name, include #1 benefit. Never exaggerate. Watch spam score.
6. **One goal per email** — Relationship-building OR call to action. Every 4th-5th email can be more sales-y.
7. **Test everything** — Segment audience, test time/day/subject

### Provider Recommendation: MailChimp [SSSS:EM.5]
Free plan available. ~$0.03/email or $10/мес for 500 subscribers. Great templates.

## The Ideal Launch [SSSS:PL.1]

### APPLY WHEN:
- Product ready (or almost) for launch
- Have been building email list for weeks/months

### 5-Step Process:

**Step 1: 6 Months Before Launch**
- Home page up: brief product description + email capture
- Drive traffic from Twitter, Facebook, blog

**Step 2: Over Next 6 Months**
- Guest blogging
- Social media updates
- Blog/forum engagement
- Always sending to home page → building list

**Step 3: 1 Week Before Launch (Tue/Wed/Thu)**
- Email list: "Product launching next week!"
- Special price ONLY for list subscribers
- Price only lasts 48 hours
- Tell exact day/time of launch email

**Step 4: Launch Day**
- Email list with special offer
- "Almost immediately, sales will start rolling in"
- Targeted mailing conversion rates: 20%+
- "A few hundred sales of your $19/мес SaaS"

**Step 5: 36 Hours After Launch**
- Final email: "Deal ends in 12 hours"
- More sales before closing special pricing

**Result:** "A simple, proven approach to increasing your launch-day sales by ten-fold."

## SEO for Startups [SSSS:SEO]

### Blog Benefits [SSSS:SEO.1]

Blogs > static websites for Google:
1. Constantly updating content = alive and relevant
2. Large quantities of content over time
3. Individual pages focused on themes (each post = its own page)
4. Clean link structures (archives, categories)

**Blog for two audiences:**
- **People** — Build trust, relationship, return visitors
- **Search engines** — Keyword optimization, free incoming traffic

### SEO Blog Strategy [SSSS:SEO.2]

**Не нужно быть originальным 100% времени.** "Writing basic posts focused on keywords and building no audience whatsoever can still be an absurdly valuable asset."

Google Sandbox: ~12-18 months to start ranking for competitive terms. Plan for this.

### Every Product Needs a Blog [SSSS:SEO.3]

"Most products you launch should have their own blog, if for nothing else than to draw search engine traffic." Especially if non-competitive market.

## PPC as Testing Tool [SSSS:MK.5]

"My recommendation for PPC is to use it for finding keywords that convert, and spend the time and money to search engine optimize for those terms."

PPC benefits: easy start, fast off, real-time adjustments.
PPC risk: costs rise over time, not sustainable long-term.

**Use PPC to:** Test keywords, validate demand, find converting terms → then SEO for those terms.

## Customer Profiles [SSSS:SW.7]

Before building sales website, profile your ideal customer:

**Demographics:**
- Married? Kids? How get to work? Office vs field? Age? Car?

**Website behavior:**
- What do they want to find?
- What can't they live without?
- Best way to convey message?
- What elements draw them in? (Audio? Video? Images?)
- What convinces them to give email? To purchase?

**Motivations:**
- What keeps them awake at night?
- What are they afraid of?
- What are they angry about?
- Top 3 daily frustrations?
- What do they desire most?
- Built-in decision bias? (engineers = analytical)
- Own jargon?
- Who else tried to sell them something similar?
