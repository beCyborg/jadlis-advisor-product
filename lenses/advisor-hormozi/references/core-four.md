# Core Four Advertising Methods — $100M Leads (Hormozi)

The only four ways to let other people know about the stuff you sell. Two audience types (warm/cold) x two communication modes (1-to-1/1-to-many) = four core methods.

**Matrix:**
- 1-to-1 + Warm Audience = **Warm Outreach** [100ML:WO]
- 1-to-many + Warm Audience = **Post Free Content** [100ML:CF]
- 1-to-1 + Cold Audience = **Cold Outreach** [100ML:CO]
- 1-to-many + Cold Audience = **Paid Ads** [100ML:PA]

**Warm Audiences** = people who gave you permission to contact them (friends, family, followers, customers, contacts).
**Cold Audiences** = people who have NOT given you permission (strangers, bought lists, other peoples' audiences).

---

## [100ML:WO] Warm Outreach

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section III, Chapter "Warm Outreach"

**Core Principle:** One-to-one contact with people who already know you. The cheapest, easiest, most reliable way to get leads. Requires $0 investment. Every business should start here. "As certain as the sun rises and sets, it works."

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Starting a business, launching a new product, $0 marketing budget, need first 5 clients, re-engaging past customers
- AVOID WHEN: You have exhausted your warm list AND are already doing other methods at scale (never truly "avoid" — always keep warm outreach going)
- COMBINES WITH: [100ML:CF] (content grows warm list for future outreach), [100ML:RF] (referrals expand warm network)

**Key Examples:**
- Hormozi's first business: texted/called/messaged friends for free fitness clients, got 6 people, built to $4K/mo
- The gym owner in Boise who desperately needed leads — Hormozi reached out to all past gym contacts and sold $60K in a day

**Actionable Framework — 10 Steps:**

### [100ML:WO.1] Get Your List
Compile ALL contacts from: phone contacts, all email accounts, all social media profiles (followers, friends, connections). Count the total. "Between your phone, email, social media, and other platforms you will have more than enough contacts to get started."

### [100ML:WO.2] Pick A Platform
Start with the platform where you have the MOST contacts. You'll hit all platforms eventually.

### [100ML:WO.3] Personalize Your Message
Use something you know about the contact as the reason to reach out. Check their social media. Don't be weird — just check in and provide value.

Example: "Saw you just had a baby! Congrats! How is the baby doing? How are you?"

### [100ML:WO.4] Reach Out to 100 People Per Day
Contact 100 people per day with personalized messages. Follow up up to 3 times (once per day for 3 days) until they respond.

### [100ML:WO.5] The ACA Framework (Warm Them Up)
When they respond, use **A-C-A**:
- **A**cknowledge — restate what they said in your own words (active listening)
- **C**ompliment — tie it to a positive character trait
- **A**sk — ask a question that leads toward your offer topic

Example (fitness): "Two kids and an accountant... Wow, supermom! So hardworking!... Do you have time to get workouts in?"

### [100ML:WO.6] Make The Offer (Do You Know Anyone?)
After 3-4 exchanges, use the Value Equation script:

"By the way, do you know anybody who is (describe struggles) looking to (dream outcome) in (time delay)? I'm taking on five case studies for free, because that's all I can handle. I just want to get some testimonials. I help them (dream outcome) without (effort and sacrifice). It works. I even guarantee people get (dream outcome) or I work with them until they do... Does anyone you like come to mind?"

**Critical feature:** You're NOT asking them to buy. You're asking if they KNOW anyone. Most who say yes volunteer themselves.

### [100ML:WO.7] Make It Free (First Five Clients)
For any new product/service — make the first five FREE. Benefits:
1. Get reps and become comfortable making offers
2. You probably suck — people are more forgiving when free
3. Learn how to suck less from real feedback
4. Get testimonials, feedback, and referrals

Free customers make money 3 ways: convert to paying, send referrals, testimonials bring paying customers.

### [100ML:WO.8] Start Back At The Top
After exhausting one platform, switch to next platform with most leads. Repeat.

### [100ML:WO.9] Start Charging
**Litmus test**: Once people start referring, start charging. Use progressive pricing: free → 80% off for next 5 → 60% off → 40% off → 20% off → full price. Keep raising by 20% every 5 clients.

### [100ML:WO.10] Keep Your List Warm — The 9-Word Email
Dean Jackson's template: "Are you still looking to [4-word desire]?"
No images. No frills. No links. Just a question. Examples:
- "Are you still looking to... buy your dream home?"
- "Are you still looking to... get more sales leads?"

**Metrics & Benchmarks:**
- 1 in 5 contacts reply (20% response rate)
- 1 in 5 who reply take the free offer (4 per 100 reach outs)
- 1 in 4 free converts to paid = ~1 customer per 100 reach outs
- 500 reach outs/week × $400 product = $104,000/year

**Reversal:**
- Limited by number of people who know you (eventually "run out")
- Limited by YOUR time (4+ hours/day minimum when starting)
- Solution: Add Content to grow warm audience, then Cold Outreach for strangers

## [100ML:CF] Post Free Content

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section III, Chapters "Post Free Content Part I" and "Part II"

**Core Principle:** One-to-many communication to your warm audience. Say it once and reach everyone. The content itself is not the compounding asset — the AUDIENCE is. Volume works. 10x the content = 10x the audience growth speed.

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: You need to grow your warm audience, want to scale beyond personal outreach, want to support all other advertising methods (78% of Gym Launch clients consumed 2+ long-form pieces BEFORE booking a call)
- AVOID WHEN: Never avoid. Even if not primary method, content makes ALL other methods work better
- COMBINES WITH: [100ML:WO] (bigger audience = more warm reach outs), [100ML:PA] (best free content becomes best paid ads), [100ML:RF] (content retains paying customers)

**Key Examples:**
- Kylie Jenner (billionaire from audience), Huda Kattan ($600M), Conor McGregor ($600M whiskey in 12 months), The Rock ($3.5B Teremana)
- Hormozi: 200K audience in 12 months → 10x content → 1.2M in next 6 months
- Hormozi's podcast took 5 YEARS to reach Top 10

### [100ML:CF.1] The Content Unit (Hook, Retain, Reward)

All content = three components:
1. **Hook** — get them to NOTICE (topic + headline + format)
2. **Retain** — get them to CONSUME (lists, steps, stories → embed questions)
3. **Reward** — SATISFY the reason they consumed it

Short content = one content unit. Long content = multiple units linked together. "There is no such thing as too long, only too boring."

**Hook Tactics — Topics (5 types):**
- Far Past: important life lessons connected to your offer
- Recent Past: meetings, conversations, client stories, testimonials
- Present: ideas as they come, tweet publicly
- Trending: apply your expertise to trending topics
- Manufactured: create experiences then document them

**Hook Tactics — Headlines (7 components from meta-analysis of news):**
Recency, Relevancy, Celebrity, Proximity, Conflict, Unusual, Ongoing

**Retain Tactics:**
- Lists: items presented one after another with a theme ("Top 10 Mistakes")
- Steps: actions in order accomplishing a goal
- Stories: describe events (real/imaginary) with lesson/takeaway

### [100ML:CF.2] Monetizing: Give-to-Ask Ratio

Television: 3.5:1 give-to-ask (47 min content, 13 min ads) — this is the MINIMUM sustainable ratio.

Growing platforms: give give give... give give give... maybe ask. Dramatically over-give and under-ask.

**Hormozi's best strategy: "Give until they ask."** People are always waiting for you to ask for money. When you don't, they trust more, share more, you grow faster. When they come to YOU, you get the BEST customers.

"Give in public, ask in private."

### [100ML:CF.3] Two Ways To Ask

1. **Integrated** — ads woven INTO content (58.5 min giving, 1.5 min asking in 1-hour podcast). Place CTAs after valuable moments or at end of content.
2. **Intermittent** — 10 "give" posts, then 1 "ask" post. Better for short-form platforms.

When asking, advertise either your lead magnet OR core offer directly.

### [100ML:CF.4] Scaling Content — Depth vs Width

**Depth-then-Width:** Maximize one platform → add another. Less resources, compounds faster, but platform risk.
**Width-then-Depth:** Get on every platform early → maximize all together. Broader reach, can repurpose, but quality may suffer.

### [100ML:CF.5] Seven Content Lessons
1. Switch from "How to" to "How I" — talk about experience, not prescriptions
2. We need to be REMINDED more than taught — repeat yourself (1 in 5 didn't know Hormozi had a book)
3. Puddles→Ponds→Lakes→Oceans — narrow focus first, expand over time
4. Content creates tools for salespeople — "greatest hits" list for sales team
5. Free content retains paying customers — buyers consume your free content too
6. People don't have shorter attention spans, they have higher standards
7. Avoid pre-scheduling posts — manual posting creates pressure to make it better

**Metrics:**
- Total followers and reach (how big)
- Rate of growth (how fast) — month over month comparison
- Posting cadence consistency
- "If your audience grows, you did good"

**Reversal:**
- Less predictable than warm outreach
- Can't personalize as well
- Competition for attention
- Must constantly innovate (copycats)

## [100ML:CO] Cold Outreach

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section III, Chapter "Cold Outreach"

**Core Principle:** One-to-one contact with strangers. More advanced cousin of warm outreach, no longer limited by warm audience. Builds on warm outreach skills. It is a numbers game — brutally effective when done with volume. "Quantity has a quality all of its own" — Napoleon.

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Want to grow faster, want predictable lead flow, want to pick exact targets (hedge fund managers, golf store owners, etc.), need independence from platform changes, want invisible-to-competitors advertising, want to build a sellable business
- AVOID WHEN: Haven't mastered warm outreach first, expect results in weeks (takes 6-12 months to scale)
- COMBINES WITH: [100ML:CF] (content warms cold leads who research you), [100ML:WO] (builds on same skills), [100ML:SC] (add employees to scale)

**Key Examples:**
- Hormozi's cold outreach hire: $0 → $32K → $64K → $96K → $160K → $224K → $320K → $480K/mo in 8 months
- Competitor doing $10M/month from ONLY cold outreach with team of 30
- Portfolio company: 4% engagement rate from email campaign for niche high-ticket service

### [100ML:CO.1] Build A List (Three Methods)

Three sources, in order of accessibility:
1. **Software scraping** — subscribe to lead scraping tools, search by criteria, test samples
2. **Brokers** — pay list brokers for targeted leads, test samples
3. **Elbow grease** — manually join groups/communities, find contact info in directories

If you can search the database, so can everyone else. Manual lists = freshest leads. More time than money → start at step 3.

### [100ML:CO.2] Personalize — Act Like You Know Them

Cold outreach must LOOK like warm outreach. 1-3 pieces of personal information + complement them + show how it benefited you.

**Cold call script structure:**
"[Name]?... (pause)... it's Alex... (pause)... I watched a few of your videos and read that blog post on [topic]. It was killer! Really helped me with [specific detail]..."

Then transition to relevant offer with value-packed lead magnet.

### [100ML:CO.3] Big Fast Value

The KEY difference: strangers give you FAR less time. "We're trying to blow their minds in under thirty seconds."

Give away stuff people ACTUALLY pay for (not "stuff they should pay for"). Hormozi's team 3x'd take rates by swapping "game planning session" (code for sales call) with actual free service delivery.

"If your offer/lead magnet isn't working, up the ante. Keep offering more until you make it so good they feel stupid saying no."

### [100ML:CO.4] Volume — Automate Delivery & Distribution

**Automated delivery:** pre-recorded voice memos, templated emails, pre-recorded voicemails, video messages
**Automated distribution:** robot dialers, email blasts, mass texting

Rule: fewer leads = less automation, more personalization. Tens of millions of potential leads = more automation OK.

### [100ML:CO.5] Follow Up — More Times, More Ways

Contact each lead multiple times through multiple channels. Email first → call to follow up on email → DM → voicemail. "Act like you're ACTUALLY trying to get ahold of these people."

After exhausting contact attempts, wait 3-6 months, then do it AGAIN (same list). Three reasons: they didn't see it, bad timing, circumstances changed.

**Seven Benefits of Cold Outreach:**
1. No content or ads creation needed — one perfect message
2. Competition won't know what you're doing (private)
3. Incredibly reliable — X inputs = Y customers
4. Fewer platform changes
5. Compliance is less painful
6. No spokesperson = sellable business
7. Hard to copy

**Metrics & Benchmarks:**
- Phone: 100 calls → 20% pickup → 25% take lead magnet = 4 engaged leads
- Email: 100 emails → 30% open → 10% reply = 3 engaged leads. Target: 3% of list → engaged leads
- DM: 100 personal videos/voice memos → 20% reply = 20 engaged leads
- Profitability threshold: LTGP ≥ 3× cost of outreach per customer

**Reversal:**
- Takes 6-12 months to scale properly
- Labor intensive
- Most people dramatically underestimate required volume
- Two prior failed attempts before success (Hormozi)

## [100ML:PA] Paid Ads

**Source:** $100M Leads (Hormozi), Section III, Chapters "Run Paid Ads Part I" and "Part II"

**Core Principle:** One-to-many to cold audiences. You trade money for reach. Fastest way to scale — reach is GUARANTEED, but getting money back isn't. A game of efficiency. "Advertising is the only casino where, with enough skill, you become the house."

**Decision Algorithm:**
- APPLY WHEN: Have money from other 3 methods, know your numbers (tracking in place), have an offer proven to convert, want fastest scaling possible
- AVOID WHEN: $0 budget, no tracking setup, haven't proven offer via warm/cold outreach first, below 3:1 LTGP:CAC
- COMBINES WITH: [100ML:CF] (best free content → best paid ads), [100ML:CO] (cold outreach teaches scripting that transfers to ads), [100ML:LM] (lead magnets lower CAC dramatically)

**Key Examples:**
- Hormozi's first ad: ALL CAPS text-only Facebook ad for gym → $1K spend → $5.7K revenue → "6 week challenge" became $1.5B+ industry promotion
- 36:1 lifetime average return on advertising
- A portfolio company took a full year of losing money before ads became profitable — then made the entire year's losses back in ONE month

### [100ML:PA.1] Pick A Platform (Four Criteria)
1. You've used it and gotten value as a consumer
2. You can target people interested in your stuff
3. You know how to format ads for the platform
4. You have minimum money to place an ad

### [100ML:PA.2] Target Your Audience (Two Methods)
1. **Lookalike audiences** — upload your customer list, platform finds similar people. Quality hierarchy: customers > warm leads > cold leads
2. **Factor targeting** — age, income, gender, interests, time, location. More filters = more specific = more efficient but smaller audience

Start narrow (efficient wins early) → expand broader (total money goes up even as efficiency drops).

### [100ML:PA.3] Make The Ad — Call Out + Value + CTA

**Call Outs (get them to NOTICE):**

Verbal call outs:
1. **Labels** — group identifiers: "Clark County Moms" "Gym Owners" "Remote Workers"
2. **Yes-Questions** — "Do you wake up to pee more than once a night?"
3. **If-Then Statements** — "If you run over $100K/mo in ads, we can save you 20%"
4. **Ridiculous Results** — "This woman lost 50 lbs eating pizza and fired her trainer"

Non-verbal call outs:
1. **Contrast** — bright colors, attractive people, movement
2. **Likeness** — look/dress/talk like your target audience ("Quack like a duck")
3. **The Scene** — visually show the problem or identity

**Value Elements — What-Who-When Framework:**

**WHAT** (8 key elements — 4 value drivers + 4 opposites):
- Dream Outcome ↔ Nightmare
- Perceived Likelihood of Achievement ↔ Risk
- Speed ↔ Time Delay
- Ease ↔ Effort and Sacrifice

**WHO** (status perspectives): Spouse, Kids, Parents, Friends, Rivals, Colleagues, Bosses — show how THEIR treatment of the customer changes

**WHEN** (timeline): Past experiences → Present reality → Future consequences. Both toward good stuff and away from bad stuff.

**CTA:**
Tell them EXACTLY what to do. Spell it out. Make it quick and easy. Short URLs. Simple actions.

### [100ML:PA.4] Get Permission — Landing Pages

Three favorite templates (simple wins). Match landing page to ad look/language. Use Cialdini's consistency principle: "Now that you did A, do B to get the most of A."

### [100ML:PA.5] Three Phases of Scaling Ads

**Phase 1: Track Money** — Set up accurate tracking before spending $1. "If you don't track, you're gonna get cleaned out."

**Phase 2: Lose Money** — Expect to lose more TIMES than you win. Budget: 2× thirty-day cash collected per customer per ad test. Imagine: $100 on 10 ads, 9 lose all $100, 1 makes $500. Down $500. Then 100× down on the winner: $10K in → $50K out. "It costs money to build an advertising machine — that's normal."

**Phase 3: Print Money** — When profitable, reverse budget from goals: "How many customers do I want?" × CAC × 1.2 (pad 20%) = daily ad budget. "If the number terrifies you, you're doing it right."

### [100ML:PA.6] LTGP:CAC Ratio & Client Financed Acquisition

**LTGP** (Lifetime Gross Profit) = all revenue from customer minus all fulfillment costs over lifetime.
**CAC** (Cost to Acquire Customer) = total advertising spend / customers acquired.
**Target: LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1.** Every struggling portfolio company has ratio < 3:1.

"The cost to acquire customers between competitors in the same industry is much closer than you'd think. The difference between winners and losers is how much they make off each customer."

**Client Financed Acquisition [100ML:PA.7]:** If customer spends more than CAC + fulfillment cost in FIRST 30 DAYS → you can recycle cash and scale infinitely. Use upsells, bundles, premium tiers to break even within 30 days. "This is how I've scaled every company past $1M/mo in the first 12 months — without outside funding."

**Metrics:**
- LTGP:CAC ratio (≥3:1 to scale)
- CAC vs industry average (below 3× average = focus on LTGP; above 3× = fix advertising)
- 30-day cash recoup rate
- Ad efficiency: spend vs engaged leads

**Reversal:**
- Requires money upfront (risk)
- Expect to lose money initially
- Ad fatigue — creative needs constant refreshing
- Platform changes can cripple overnight
- Don't confuse sales problems with advertising problems (qualified leads not buying = sales problem, not ad problem)
- Best free content often makes the best paid ads (User Generated Content especially)

## Cross-Method Integration

**Hormozi's recommended order for new businesses:**
1. Warm outreach (always first, $0)
2. Post free content (in addition to warm outreach)
3. Cold outreach (when you want faster/more predictable growth)
4. Paid ads (last — requires money and skills from other 3)

**Time vs Money decision:**
- More time than money → content + outreach
- More money than time → cold outreach + paid ads

**All methods compound together:** A business doing content + paid ads gets MORE from each than a business doing only one. Cold outreach + content = better cold outreach AND better warm leads. "Every combination of the core four advertising activities boost each other in some way."

**Hormozi's personal track record:**
- First business: content + warm outreach
- Gyms: free content + paid ads
- Gym Launch: paid ads + cold outreach
- Prestige Labs: affiliates
- ALAN: paid ads + affiliates
- Acquisition.com: posting content

"They all work if you do."
